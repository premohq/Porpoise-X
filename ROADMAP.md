# Porpoise roadmap

Notes for what comes after 4.5. Not promises, just the plan so far.

## Side menu (quick menu)

- Scroll up past the top rows to the Controllers and Camera cards. Opening one
  sets up controllers right there. The controller settings get redone from
  scratch for this.
- Scroll up past the cards to swipe into a theme picker, with a live preview of
  each: the new Porpoise look (selected), Revolution, and Star Cube.
- Full Settings stays at the bottom of the side menu.

## Other themes

- 4.5 brought the side menu, the island and the new in-game menu to
  Revolution, Star Cube and Porpoise Legacy. Next: their own touches on top,
  like the theme picker above.
- Porpoise Legacy will be phased out later.

## Settings

- OLED and the rest of the look options stay in Settings > Interface.
- Controller settings: redone along with the side menu cards above.

## Still open from testing

- DualSense battery level (reading the right byte from the logs).
- Rumble and light bar check, stick drift.
- PS3 Eye dropping off USB.
- Light gun games, per-player tabs, PlayStation Move button remapping.
- GTA IV bug.
- Translations for the new 4.5 text.
